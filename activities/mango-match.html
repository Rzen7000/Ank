🧑‍🏫 Teacher Tips & Extensions

  • Warm-Up: Use as a 5-minute knowledge hook about Egyptian fruits.
  • Research Extension: Have students research additional facts about each fruit.
  • Vocabulary Building: Introduce words like "potassium," "fibre," and "nutrient."
  • Geography Connection: Discuss why these fruits grow well in Egypt.
  • Creative Writing: Students write a story featuring one of the fruits.
  • Reflection: Ask: "Which fruit fact surprised you the most? Why?"
🔥 Challenge Mode: Students must write their own fruit facts and trade with a partner to match them!
