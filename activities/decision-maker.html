🧑‍🏫 Teacher Tips & Extensions

  • Warm-Up: Use as a 5-minute hook to introduce a debate topic.
  • Persuasive Writing: Have students write a paragraph arguing for or against the Committee's decision.
  • Class Debate: Split the class into "Pro-Committee" and "Anti-Committee" teams.
  • Creative Extension: Students write a short story about why the Committee made that decision.
  • Vocabulary Builder: Introduce words like "justify," "evidence," "perspective," and "counterargument."
  • Reflection: Ask: "How did you decide whether to agree or disagree? What influenced your thinking?"
🔥 Challenge Mode: Students must argue the opposite of their true opinion, practicing perspective-taking and empathy.
