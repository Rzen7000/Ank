🧑‍🏫 Teacher Tips & Extensions

  • Warm-Up: Use the generator as a 5-minute writing warm-up.
  • Story Structure: Teach beginning, middle, and end using prompts.
  • Character Development: Have students create character profiles for their stories.
  • Peer Review: Students share and give feedback on each other's stories.
  • Vocabulary Building: Challenge students to use new vocabulary in their stories.
  • Reflection: Ask: "What was the most challenging part of writing your story?"
🔥 Challenge Mode: Students must include all 5 story elements (Character, Setting, Conflict, Plot, Resolution) in their story!
