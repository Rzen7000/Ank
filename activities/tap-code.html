🧑‍🏫 Teacher Tips & Extensions

  • Warm-Up: Use as a 5-minute introduction to Morse code.
  • Pattern Recognition: Discuss how dots and dashes form patterns.
  • History Connection: Talk about the history of Morse code.
  • Partner Work: Students tap messages to each other.
  • Vocabulary: Introduce "encoding," "decoding," "pattern," and "signal."
  • Reflection: Ask: "What strategies helped you remember the codes?"
🔥 Challenge Mode: Students must tap a secret message using Morse code and have a partner decode it!
