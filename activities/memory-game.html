🧑‍🏫 Teacher Tips & Extensions

  • Warm-Up: Use as a 5-minute focus exercise.
  • Memory Strategies: Discuss techniques like chunking and association.
  • Partner Play: Students take turns flipping cards and discuss strategy.
  • Pattern Recognition: Talk about how our brains recognize patterns.
  • Reflection: Ask: "What strategy helped you remember card positions?"
  • Creative Extension: Students create their own card pairs with their own emojis.
🔥 Challenge Mode: Students must describe the location of a card using grid coordinates (e.g., "Row 2, Column 3").
