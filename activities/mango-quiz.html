🥭 The Mango Committee

🎯 Self-Reflection 🧠 Personality ⭐ Level 1

Discover your Imaginarium personality! The Mango Committee will judge you. (In a nice way.)

📖 Learning Objective Encourage self-reflection and discussion about personal traits, preferences, and how we relate to characters and stories. ⏱️ 10–15 min

🧑‍🏫 Teacher Tips & Extensions

  • Icebreaker: Use as a fun way to start discussions about personality.
  • Character Connection: Discuss how each personality type relates to the story characters.
  • Writing Prompt: Students write about a time they embodied their personality type.
  • Class Discussion: Share results and discuss similarities and differences.
  • Reflection: Ask: "Do you agree with your result? Why or why not?"
  • Creative Extension: Students create their own quiz questions for the Mango Committee.
🔥 Challenge Mode: Students must argue for a different personality type than their result, practicing perspective-taking.
