🧑‍🏫 Teacher Tips & Extensions

  • Student Voice: Let students create and debate their own topics!
  • Persuasive Writing: Have students write a full paragraph defending their vote.
  • Class Debate: Split the class into "For" and "Against" teams for each topic.
  • Counterargument: Challenge students to argue the opposite of their vote.
  • Vocabulary: Teach "claim," "evidence," "warrant," "rebuttal," and "fallacy."
  • Reflection: Ask: "What made you choose your side? Did Bennu's verdict change your mind?"
🔥 Challenge Mode: Students must write a counterargument to their own position, then share both with a partner.
