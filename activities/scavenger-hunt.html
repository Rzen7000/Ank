🔍 Bennu's Scavenger Hunt

🎯 Geography 🧠 Discovery ⭐ Level 2

Follow the clues, find the locations. Bennu is your guide. (He's not very helpful.)

📖 Learning Objective Develop geographic knowledge and critical thinking skills by solving riddles about famous Egyptian locations and earning a certificate. ⏱️ 20–35 min

📜 Game History

0 entries

🧑‍🏫 Teacher Tips & Extensions

  • Geography Connection: Use as an introduction to Egyptian landmarks.
  • Research Extension: Students research one location in depth.
  • Map Skills: Have students plot the locations on a map.
  • Creative Writing: Students write their own clues for a location.
  • Partner Work: Students discuss clues in pairs before answering.
  • Reflection: Ask: "Which location would you most like to visit? Why?"
🔥 Challenge Mode: Students must create their own scavenger hunt with 5 clues about local landmarks!
