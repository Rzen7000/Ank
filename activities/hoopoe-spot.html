🧑‍🏫 Teacher Tips & Extensions

  • Warm-Up: Use as a 5-minute focus exercise.
  • Observation Skills: Discuss strategies for finding hidden objects in chaotic scenes.
  • Pattern Recognition: Talk about how Bennu blends into each scene.
  • Descriptive Language: Have students describe where Bennu was hiding.
  • Patience & Persistence: Great for developing "stick-with-it-ness."
  • Reflection: Ask: "What strategies helped you find Bennu faster?"
🔥 Challenge Mode: Students must describe Bennu's location using positional language (e.g., "He's in the top-right corner, near the palm tree").
