🧑‍🏫 Teacher Tips & Extensions

  • Warm-Up: Use as a 5-minute brain teaser activity.
  • Critical Thinking: Discuss strategies for solving riddles.
  • Vocabulary Building: Introduce words like "metaphor," "analogy," and "wordplay."
  • Creative Extension: Students write their own riddles for classmates to solve.
  • Partner Work: Students discuss riddles in pairs before answering.
  • Reflection: Ask: "What strategy helped you solve the riddles?"
🔥 Challenge Mode: Students must write their own riddle and trade with a partner to solve each other's!
