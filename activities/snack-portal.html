🎒 Jimba's Snack Portal

🎯 Decision-Making 📖 Storytelling ⭐ Level 2

Jimba's backpack is a portal to another dimension. Today, you're going inside. Bring snacks.

📖 Learning Objective Develop decision-making skills, explore consequences of choices, and engage in creative storytelling through an interactive adventure. ⏱️ 15–25 min

📜 Adventure History

0 entries

🧑‍🏫 Teacher Tips & Extensions

  • Decision-Making: Discuss the consequences of each choice.
  • Creative Writing: Students write an alternate ending.
  • Character Study: Discuss Jimba, Bennu, and the Mango Committee.
  • Vocabulary: Introduce words like "consequence," "alternate," and "perspective."
  • Partner Work: Students discuss choices before clicking.
  • Reflection: Ask: "What would you have done differently? Why?"
🔥 Challenge Mode: Students must write their own adventure path with 3-4 choices and trade with a partner!
